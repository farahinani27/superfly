Return &Refund policy

How a return works

  1. 01

    Pack it

    Original packaging, in the original shipping container, with the receipt.

  2. 02

    Ship it back

    Send it to the return address below. Keep your tracking number.

  3. 03

    We check it

    The goods are checked against the receipt and confirmed.

  4. 04

    Refund in 15 days

    Paid within 15 days of the confirmation.

What is refundable

Refundable

  • Returned with the receipt
  • Goods in good condition
  • Packaging in original condition
  • In the original shipping container as received

Not refundable

  • Goods used by the customer
  • Goods damaged by the customer
  • Shipments lost during the return transit — Superflymotos is not responsible for these

The policy in full

The returned goods/products will be checked with the receipt together and the goods should be in the good condition. The packaging should also be in original condition in the original shipping container as received. If the goods/products has been used or damaged by the customer, there will not be any refund. Superflymotos will also not be responsible if your shipment is lost during the return transit. Once we have checked and confirm the goods/products, customer will received their refund within 15 days after the confirmation.
