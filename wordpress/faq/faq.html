FrequentlyAsked questions

Ordering & delivery

How do I place an order?

The whole order takes a few minutes.

  1. Press Add to cart on the products you want.
  2. Press Continue shopping to keep browsing, or Checkout when you are done.
  3. Sign in, or check out as a guest — an account lets you track the order later.
  4. Fill in your billing and shipping address, then pick a shipping method.
  5. Choose a payment method.
  6. Review the order, agree to the terms and press Place order.

You will get a confirmation email the moment the order is received. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder before contacting us.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes — but only while the order is still pending. Email superflymotosofficial@gmail.com with the subject Request to cancel order and your order number.

Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled. See the Return & Refund Policy instead.

How long does delivery take?

Normally 7 to 14 working days from the day payment clears. Working days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Payment & pricing

What payment methods are available?

Card and online banking — Visa, Mastercard and Malaysian internet banking, handled by our payment provider at checkout.

Bank transfer — pay the invoice total into our account, then email us the transfer details: bank name, date, time, reference number, amount paid and your order number. Send the confirmation to superflymotosofficial@gmail.com.

Bank-transfer orders are not shipped until we have confirmed the payment.

What currency are prices in?

Every transaction is in Ringgit Malaysia (RM). A price shown in another currency is a conversion for reference only — your bank is charged the RM amount.

Your account

How do I change my details or shipping address?

Sign in and open My account. Account details holds your name, email and password; Addresses holds your billing and shipping addresses.

Changing an address here does not change an order that has already been placed — email us for that.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
  1. On the login page, press Lost your password?
  2. Enter the email address you registered with and submit.
  3. Open the reset link we email you and choose a new password.
